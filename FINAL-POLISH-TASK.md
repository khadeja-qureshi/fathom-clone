\# Final Product Polish Task



Do a final product-polish and demo-readiness pass on the existing Throughline app.



Do not redesign or rebuild the product. Preserve the existing product thesis, routes, seeded data and working interactions.



\## Priorities



\### 1. Improve desktop presentation

\- Increase usable content width where appropriate.

\- Reduce excessive empty margins on large screens.

\- Improve information density without making the UI cramped.

\- Improve readability of headings, metadata, buttons and transcript content.



\### 2. Make the meeting workspace the strongest screen

\- Strengthen the relationship between playback, transcript, summary, decisions, actions and clips.

\- Make source timestamps and jump-to-source controls obviously interactive.

\- Ensure transcript mode is readable and useful for the long meeting.

\- Make actions, decisions and highlights feel like first-class objects.

\- Preserve all working state changes and source navigation.



\### 3. Improve meetings library

\- Make cards easier to scan.

\- Improve hierarchy for participants, duration, decisions, action counts and summaries.

\- Preserve short, medium and 8-person long seeded meetings.



\### 4. Improve evidence-backed search

\- Make results feel like evidence rather than generic cards.

\- Emphasize meeting, speaker, timestamp, matched excerpt and jump-to-source.

\- Preserve existing working query/filter behavior.



\### 5. Verify public sharing

\- Ensure at least one full-meeting public share route works without authentication.

\- Ensure a highlight/clip share experience works if already implemented.

\- Public pages should look intentional and polished.



\### 6. Do not add authentication

The assignment requires the deployed application to open for somebody who is not signed in.



Keep the project as a public seeded demo workspace.



A subtle "Demo workspace / capture stubbed" indicator is enough.



\### 7. Verify visible controls

Fix, remove, or clearly disable any prominent control that does nothing.



\### 8. Responsive quality

Desktop is the priority, but the app should remain usable at normal laptop widths.



\### 9. Technical verification

Run the production build and fix build, type or runtime problems encountered.



\### 10. Documentation

Review and update:

\- README.md

\- PRODUCT-DECISIONS.md

\- WALKTHROUGH.md



They must match the final implementation.



Do not modify:

\- .agent-logs/

\- CAPTURE-TEST.md

\- jules-8x.ps1

\- .cursor/

\- any agent-capture infrastructure



\## Final report



Report:

\- UI/UX improvements made

\- interactions verified

\- public share routes available

\- production build result

\- anything still incomplete
